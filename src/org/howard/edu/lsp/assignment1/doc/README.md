# Assignment 1 - Course Orientation

1. **Are late programming assignments accepted?**

   No. Late assignments are not accepted. All required work must be submitted by the assigned due date and time.

2. **Where will official course announcements be posted?**

   Official announcements will be posted on Piazza.

3. **How will examination dates be announced?**

   Midterm and final examination dates will be announced in class and posted on Piazza at least two weeks in advance.

4. **Under what circumstances will a makeup examination be considered?**

   A makeup examination will be considered only under extraordinary circumstances beyond the student's control. The student must notify the instructor as soon as reasonably possible and provide appropriate supporting documentation.

5. **Is AI permitted in this course?**

   Generative AI may be used only when the instructor or a specific assignment permits it. Students remain responsible for the accuracy, originality, testing, documentation, and understanding of submitted work and must be able to explain, modify, debug, and defend it.

6. **Which course policy will have the greatest impact on your success this semester? Explain why.**

   The requirement to monitor Piazza will have the greatest impact on my success because assignment updates, clarifications, and deadline changes may be posted there. Checking it consistently will help me avoid missing changes and allow me to ask questions early.

7. **When are office hours for LSP Fall 2026?**

   Office hours are held by Zoom appointment on an as-needed basis.

8. **What is your responsibility for monitoring Piazza, and are you responsible for assignment updates, clarifications, or deadline changes posted there?**

   I am responsible for monitoring Piazza throughout the semester. I am also responsible for following assignment updates, clarifications, and deadline changes posted there.

9. **What documentation is required if you are unable to take an examination because of a medical issue or other approved circumstance?**

   The student must provide appropriate supporting documentation verifying that they could not participate at the scheduled time. It need not disclose a diagnosis or other private medical information, and a doctor's note, urgent care receipt, or medical-visit verification does not automatically qualify the student for a makeup examination.

10. **Does scholarship status entitle a student to different grading, deadline, attendance, or course requirements?**

    No. Scholarship status does not entitle a student to different grading, deadlines, attendance expectations, or course requirements.
